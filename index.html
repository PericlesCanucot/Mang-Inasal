Born in Iloilo. Loved by All Filipinos.

Mang Inasal started in 2003 in Iloilo City with a simple dream: to serve the best chicken inasal in the country. Today, we have grown to over 500 branches nationwide, becoming a staple in every Filipino's heart.

Known for our unique secret marinade and the beloved unlimited rice offering, we continue to bring joy and satisfaction to millions of families every single day.
